## The Newsroom

This show aired in 2012, but I didn't get to it until 2026. After finishing the first episode, I realized that the "iconic scene" I'd come across online had originated here — anchor Will McAvoy, at a Northwestern University forum, asked by a sophomore why America is the greatest country in the world, and then delivering that monologue.

The reason I want to recommend it is that the existence of OneOverX, and my own approach to creating, came in some part from *The Newsroom*: **put things in context, be accountable to facts, don't chase trending topics or make content just to please an audience, hold your own opinions while presenting dissenting ones.**

In an age when journalism itself has become something of an ideal — when algorithms keep manufacturing "content that flatters demand and drives clicks" — I find myself missing something classical and traditional. Something stubborn, something that has been ground and refined through friction. That's what gives me a sense of warmth.
