## Different forms of work

- **Onsite/In-Office**: The traditional model. You show up at a physical location, work fixed hours, and are embedded in an organizational structure. Identity is often tied to the company.
- **Remote Work:** Work from anywhere - home, cafe, or another country. Enabled by digital tools. Became mainstream post-2020. Highy competitive to land in 2025 due to global talent pools.
- **Hybrid Work**: Split time between office and remote. The dominant corporate model in 2025. Attempts to balance collaboration with flexibility.
- **Freelancer/Self-Employed**: You are the business. Sell skills to multiple clients on a project basis.  Maximum autonomy, but you carry all the risk- income, taxes, benefits, and client acquisition are all on you.
- **Part-Time Employment**: Regular employment uner 35 hours/week. Often used as a bridge-students, caregivers, or people building a side business. Provides stability with more time for other pursuits.
- **Solopreneur/Creator**: Build a one-person business around your knowledge, content, or products. No boss, no clients in the traditional sense-you build an audience or system that generates income. The "new freelancer".

PS: two traps about stability and freedom
- The stability trap: stability feels safe but can become a cage. Many people trade time for security without realizing the cost-until they want out.
- The freedom trap: Freedom without income systems is just stress. The goal is building freedom that's backed by reliable cash flow - not just excaping a job.

## viable job direction

There are many subreddits related to making money, like r/sidehustlegold, r/AIIncomeLab, r/PassiveIncome, etc.

We all want to make money, even make some tricky money, make quick money.
But we have to accept, it needs a long time to build foudation that money like water flows to you.

There are three facts you have to learn about:
- opportunities for junior is less than senior. But it is related to working time, you can win the game if you are strong enough.
- AI doesn't kill the competition. Content creation, coding, designing... still have demands. And because of AI, some new positions come up, eg: AI automation, AI solution architect, and even facelss video relieves the possibility for many people.
- If you want to start a business on your own, the first lesson we should learn is not niche worth investing, not tools should learn, but do one thing consistantly in a long term.

On the Internet, it is easy to look such posts: I made XX in one month through doing XX, I started from scratch and I made XX now. 
If you still click such posts, it doesn't mind. If you quit things you started last week and do a new thing because of this post, it doesn't mind.
It does mind that one day you will realise money cannot flow to you, if you still continues the cycle.

But it delivers some signals that you can enter into this niche, which still has money to earn, only if you carefuly do this.
I have to say, don't underrate the difficulty of anything, don't overrate the difficulty of anything. Most things don't require high EQ.

These are some viable jobs.
- AI automation: automate manual work, tools including make, zapier, n8n, AI agents.
- Ghostwriter: create consistent, brand-related contents for founders, creators and others on linkedin, website and other platforms.
- Website designer: the difference from AI generation is design and copy which can improve high conversion, tools including wordpress, webflow, framer...
- SEO: how to make products/brands rank high in google search results. AI produces new demands about AI SEO optimization, making products/brands mentioned by ai and optimizing Ai answers about products/brands;
- paid niche newsletter: profits through subscription, sponsorship and affiliate marketing.
- copywriter: including landing page, social media, products and others, the key is to improve conversion;
- Email marketing: including automated email sequence, high-conversion copy, user labels and ab test;
- Digital products: templates, mini courses...
- Techincal doc specialist: convert complex system into clear and structured guides, sops and internal wikis.
- Scripwriter for faceless video: good scripts and audio is the key to faceless video.
- consultants: provide professional advice and strategy
- solo developer: develop mini SaaS for a specific and small problem, get subscription fees.
