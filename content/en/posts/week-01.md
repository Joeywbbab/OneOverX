As long as x is finite, 1/x never equals zero.

It gets small. Small enough to nearly disappear. But it is never nothing — the gap between the hyperbola and the axis that never quite closes.

This weekly is built in that gap.

## Three columns

- **beta** — what I'm building, still unfinished.
- **X** — one idea worth turning over.
- **1·x** — a slice of a life lived off the default path.

Once a week. Staying non-zero.
