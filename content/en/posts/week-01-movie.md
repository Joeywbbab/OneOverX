## Off Campus

A genuinely easy-on-the-eyes show, adapted from the North American romance novel *The Deal*. With an all-women creative team behind it, it delivers a refreshingly wholesome take on romance — affectionately roasted online as "basically science fiction." Season one has wrapped.
{{< /media-item >}}

## Not Suitable for Work

Follows five young people trying to make it in Manhattan. Each is grinding away at their own career, with a bit of relationship drama woven through. I didn't expect much going in, but it's turned out to be genuinely good. It's written by the same writer behind *Never Have I Ever*, and it's currently airing.
{{< /media-item >}}
